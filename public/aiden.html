Methodology
Transparent and defensible assessment logic

Contextual weighting

Dimension weights and minimum targets change according to AI ambition. Autonomous adoption places greater emphasis on governance, observability, controls and AI operations.

Evidence confidence

Evidence is mapped to controls with confidence. Missing evidence lowers confidence rather than automatically proving that a capability does not exist.

Linked indices

Enterprise AI readiness and QE adoption readiness remain separate so leadership can distinguish enterprise foundations from domain execution capability.
